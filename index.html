About this tool

Purpose

Built by the Seton Catholic Preparatory Athletic Department to support a formal region realignment proposal to the AIA for the 3A conference starting 2026–27. The current alignment forces excessive travel — this tool proposes a 7-region solution limiting most intra-region trips to under 90 miles.

Schedule structure

  • 18 games per school (15–16 for Yuma region due to geography)
  • 12 region games — full home-and-away round robin
  • 2 away long-haul + 2 home long-haul — 2-year rotating contracts
  • 2–4 non-region nearby games
  • 9 home / 9 away — perfectly balanced
  • Max 3 consecutive home or away — no streaks longer than 3
  • No games: Sundays · Thanksgiving weekend · Dec 18–Jan 1

Playoff alignment

Added 4A schools (Coconino, Flagstaff, Lee Williams, Prescott, Bradshaw Mtn, Kofa, Somerton) and 2A schools (Many Farms, Pinon, Hopi, Valley/Sanders, Parker, Kingman Academy, Mohave Accel.) participate in region play for scheduling purposes but compete in their own 4A or 2A conference playoffs — not the 3A bracket. Their non-region games are scheduled against same-class opponents only.

Boys / Girls coordination

When the girls play at home, the boys travel — and vice versa. The boys schedule mirrors these H/A assignments on the same dates.
